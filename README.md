📊 End-to-End Sales Analytics & Business Intelligence

An end-to-end Sales Analytics and Business Intelligence project using SQL, Python, and Microsoft Power BI to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights and an interactive dashboard.

🚀 Project Overview

This project analyzes Super Store sales data to understand overall business performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, regional performance, and sales trends.

The project follows a complete analytics workflow:

Raw Data → SQL → Python → Power BI → Business Insights

🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profitability
- Identify high-performing regions and categories
- Analyze customer segment performance
- Identify top-performing products and cities
- Track monthly sales and profit trends
- Create interactive KPIs and visualizations
- Generate actionable business insights

🛠️ Tech Stack
